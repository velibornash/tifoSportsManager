# TIFO Football Manager — User Manual

This manual covers the graphical football manager opened from the sports lobby.

## 1. Login and entering the football game

1. Open the application and sign in with your account.
2. The Sports Lobby appears after a successful login.
3. Select **TIFO UI MANAGER** with the football icon.
4. The football dashboard opens.

The other lobby cards open the text football, basketball and American football modes.

## 2. Dashboard

The dashboard is the starting screen for your club. It shows:

- your club name and logo;
- current league position, points, record and goal difference;
- your next fixture;
- recent results;
- club milestones such as top scorer, top assist, biggest win, biggest loss and attendance;
- important updates about your club, matches, medical cases, transfers and messages;
- the current game date and season phase.

Clicking the league summary opens the league table. Clicking the next match opens the match preview.

## 3. Header options

### Sports Lobby

Returns to the sports selection screen, where you can choose another game mode.

### Club

Opens your club area. From there you can manage your squad, fixtures, training, finances and club
operations.

### League

Opens your current league. You can view the table, schedule, results, clubs and players in the league.

### Country

Opens your country page. The country page contains the national football structure, domestic leagues,
domestic cup, playoffs and national teams when they are available.

### World

Shows all countries in the game, their rating and whether they are active or simulated. It also gives
access to international club competitions and country pages.

### Community

Opens the community area. It contains the forum and private messages.

### Notifications

Opens your notification list. Notifications can come from private messages, forum activity and other
manager actions. You can mark individual notifications or all notifications as read.

### Account menu

The account menu contains:

- **My profile** — opens your manager profile;
- **Club settings** — opens club profile and stadium-related settings when available;
- **Sign out** — ends the current session and returns to login.

### Admin

This option is visible only to administrators, owners and development users. It opens the administration
area described in §11.

## 4. Club options

The Club area contains the following options.

### First Team

Shows your senior squad. Open a player to see their skills, position, form, contract, medical status,
match history and transfer actions.

### Schedule

Shows your club's upcoming fixtures and their competition, round, date, venue and home/away status.

### Results

Shows matches that have already been played. Open a match to see the score, events, lineups, statistics,
goals, reports and replay options.

### Juniors

Opens the youth academy. You can review junior prospects, academy quality, the junior school, carryover
players and archived prospects. Depending on the season stage, you can promote, retain or release
prospects.

### Tactics

Opens the formation and tactical setup for your team. Use it to choose the formation, assign players to
positions and save tactical rules used by the match simulation.

### Tactic Editor

Provides detailed tactical rules for different match situations, including possession, defensive shape,
roles and positioning. Save the profile after changing it.

### Staff

Shows your club staff and available staff information. Staff can affect club management and training
systems.

### Finances

Shows the club budget, squad value, weekly wage bill, income, expenses, net balance, financial ledger,
board concerns and board praise.

### Transfers

Opens the transfer centre. You can:

- browse listed players;
- filter the market by country;
- show interest in a player;
- buy a listed player;
- list one of your players;
- inspect incoming offers;
- accept or reject offers where available;
- withdraw a listing or clear interest;
- open a player's transfer details.

### Training

Opens the weekly training setup. Configure the club's training plan and intensity, then review how the
plan affects player development, fatigue and injury risk.

### Training Reports

Shows completed weekly training reports for the club and individual players.

### Medical Center

Shows injured players, injury details and recovery actions. It helps you decide whether a player is
ready for selection.

### Club Profile

Shows the public club identity, manager, league, squad overview, stadium information and club milestones.
Other managers can reach a club's manager profile from the club page.

### Stadium

Shows the ground, capacity, seating sectors, prices, pitch condition, maintenance budget, training
facilities and stadium image. Use it to manage the ground and fund pitch work.

### Friendlies

Shows friendly match requests and friendly-related information. The backend supports friendly requests,
but some invitation and free-slot actions are still being completed.

## 5. League options

### League Table

Shows the standings for the selected season. The table includes position, played matches, wins, draws,
losses, goals and points. Select a club to open its profile.

### League Schedule

Shows the league rounds and scheduled fixtures for the selected season.

### League Results

Shows played results from the league. Open a match for its full match page.

### League Club

Opens a selected club from the table or schedule. You can inspect its profile, squad, fixtures and
manager link where available.

### Player Statistics

Shows player match statistics and player histories. Some aggregate statistics screens are still being
refined, so an empty result may mean that the underlying statistic has not been populated yet.

### Team Statistics

Shows team-level statistics and comparisons for the selected competition or season.

### Top Scorers and Top Assists

Shows the leading goal scorers and assist providers for the selected competition or season.

## 6. Country options

The Country option opens your own country. The country label may include the country's name and flag.

### Country overview

Shows the country's football structure, active status, national rating and available competitions.

### Domestic leagues

Shows the divisions in the country. Open a division to view its table, fixtures, results and clubs.

### Domestic cup

Shows the country's cup fixtures, rounds and results. Open a fixture or match for more detail.

### Playoffs

Shows promotion or relegation playoff information when the country has a playoff path for the current
season.

### Senior national team

Shows the senior national squad and national-team information. National-team management, appointments,
elections and squad selection are separate from your club squad.

### U-21 national team

Shows the U-21 national-team area when the relevant squad and competition data exist.

### Simulated countries

The World page can open simulated countries. These countries may have a national record and static club
structure without active club squads or played domestic fixtures.

## 7. World options

The World page provides a global view of the football world.

### Country ranking

Lists all countries with their rating, ranking position and state. **Active** means the country has a
fully activated football structure. **Simulated** means the country is represented in the world but may
not have active players and fixtures.

### Champions Cup

Opens the international club Champions Cup. Select a tier to view its groups, played results and
knockout bracket.

### Masters Cup

Opens the international club Masters Cup with the same tier, group and bracket navigation.

### Challenge Cup

Opens the international club Challenge Cup with its tier, group, result and bracket views.

### National competitions

The World page lists national qualifying and World Cup competitions as unavailable rows. The backend
structure exists, but these competitions cannot yet be opened from the graphical UI until the national
tournament screen is finished.

## 8. Match pages

A match page can be opened from the dashboard, schedule, results, league pages or player history.

### Preview

Shows the upcoming fixture, teams, venue, competition and available pre-match information.

### Lineups

Shows the selected players and team shape for the match.

### Match statistics

Shows team and player statistics from the played match, including possession, shots, passes, duels and
other recorded events.

### Goals and events

Shows goals, cards, injuries, substitutions, fouls, penalties and other match events.

### Match report

Shows the generated post-match report and summary.

### Replay

Opens the match replay viewer when replay data is available.

### Preview and report

Shows the pre-match and post-match analytical views.

## 9. Community options

### Forum

The forum has two sections: **TIFO** and **GENERAL**.

You can:

- create a topic;
- open a topic and read replies;
- reply to a topic;
- edit your own post;
- delete your own post;
- follow a thread using the previous/next navigation.

Moderators, administrators and owners can edit or delete other managers' posts. A forum ban prevents a
manager from writing in the forum while leaving reading, messaging and game access available.

### Messages

Private messages are organised into conversation threads. You can:

- choose another account;
- start a conversation with a subject and message;
- reply to an existing conversation;
- read the full thread;
- open a conversation from a notification.

## 10. Manager profiles

### My profile

Shows your manager account information and your linked club. You can update the display name.

### Public manager profile

Shows another manager's public name, role, club, league and country. Email address, username and last
seen information are not shown.

Moderators can reach forum-ban actions from the relevant manager profile.

## 11. Admin options

The Admin option is available only to authorised staff.

### Accounts

Review registration requests, approve or reject applicants, assign staff roles, repair club links and
apply or lift forum bans.

### Database operations

- **Reset DB** — clears football data while preserving the owner account and tactic-editor setups.
- **Initialize DB** — builds the Serbian football structure with clubs, players and fixtures.
- **Seed other nations** — builds static structures for countries that are not activated.
- **Repair world** — repairs the country catalogue and national-team baseline.
- **Re-seed national teams** — fills missing national-team squads.
- **Re-draw the cup** — draws missing domestic cup rounds.
- **Repair international cups** — creates the 15 international club cup rows and fills missing simulated-
  country club structures. Existing data is kept.
- **Seed national tournaments** — creates or draws national qualifying and tournament state.
- **Reset national ratings** — applies the explicit national-rating correction operation.

Database operations show a progress dialog and should be allowed to finish. Starting the application does
not perform these operations automatically.

### Clock and jobs

Administrators can inspect job definitions and runs and manually advance the game hour, day or week in
development. These controls change the shared game world.

## 12. Mobile navigation

On a phone, open the menu button to show the side menu. The same areas are grouped into expandable
sections:

- Dashboard;
- Club;
- League;
- Country;
- World;
- Community;
- Admin, for authorised users;
- Sports Lobby;
- My profile and Sign out.

The live game clock is also shown in the mobile menu.

## 13. Important terms

- **Season** — a twelve-week game period, numbered from 1.
- **Fixture** — a scheduled match that may not have been played yet.
- **Match** — the persisted result of a played fixture.
- **Active country** — a country with its playable football structure activated.
- **Simulated country** — a country represented in the world with limited or lazy-built football data.
- **Club reputation** — the club's economy/strength scale from 0 to 100.
- **Club Elo** — the separate rating used for club ranking updates.
