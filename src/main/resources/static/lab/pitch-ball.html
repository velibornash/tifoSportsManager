Pitch & Ball — positions from the Tactic Editor

Drag the white ball. Both teams play the SAME saved tactic: the blue HOME side reads it directly, the red AWAY side reads it mirrored exactly like the app transforms it for the away perspective (TacticalPerspectiveTransformer: row' = 9 − row, col' = 8 − col). Circles glide at the match engine's pace-capped speed (pace 20 = 0.75 cells/tick) and touch without ever overlapping. The checkboxes run the engine's match behaviors: Carrier is the owned-ball flag (uncheck it to release the ball), CHASE_BALL sends the nearest of each team at a loose ball (first there takes it, arriving together = duel), THREAT A presses the carrier — the press ends in a tackle duel, THREAT B presses a dangerous opponent without the ball. Duels use the engine's own odds: equal players = 50/50, winner takes the ball, both wait 3.7 s (7 ticks) before dueling again. On a duel (or a clean chase claim) the winner moves onto the ball's position, the loser and everyone else freeze in place — all movement stops.
